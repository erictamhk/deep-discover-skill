# Report — 催眠是真實的意識狀態改變，還是社會順從？(2023-2025 科學證據)

## Direct answer (synthesis position)

**The 2023-2025 evidence does not support either extreme. It supports a synthesis: hypnosis is neither a "special trance state" in the strong traditional sense, nor merely social compliance/role-playing.** Both strict poles of the classical debate are disconfirmed by converging evidence, and the most recent theoretical work treats the dichotomy itself as false (EV-0008, EV-0034, C-0005, C-0004). The honest reading of the current literature is a three-part answer:

1. **Measurable brain changes ARE real** — multiple 2023-2025 fMRI/EEG/MRS studies reliably find hypnosis-related changes in parieto-occipital/cuneal connectivity, interhemispheric frontoparietal connectivity, theta enhancement, and DMN/SN/ECN modulation (EV-0001, EV-0002, EV-0003, EV-0004, EV-0019; C-0001). These are not artifacts of compliance — faking subjects behave differently from genuinely highly-suggestible ones (EV-0007).

2. **But there is NO hypnosis-specific, unique physiological fingerprint**, and induction effects are small and often mediated by expectancy and the trait of hypnotizability rather than by a state per se (EV-0005, EV-0006, EV-0007, EV-0008, EV-0009, EV-0016, EV-0020; C-0002). The strong "trance required" (qualitative-state) hypothesis is largely ruled out (EV-0008, EV-0016; C-0005).

3. **Causal (TMS/tDCS) evidence shows frontal mechanisms are genuinely causally involved, beyond mere expectancy** — inhibiting the left DLPFC reliably increases hypnotizability across an RCT and multiple tDCS studies (EV-0029, EV-0030, EV-0031; C-0007). This weakens the pure-epiphenomenal/compliance reading. However, it does NOT adjudicate which specific theory is correct, because the two leading theories (cold-control and dissociated-control) make the *same* directional prediction for this manipulation (EV-0015; C-0006, C-0007).

**The emerging 2022-2025 consensus dissolves the dichotomy**: predictive coding / active inference provides a unified framework where "state-like" precision-weighting and "non-state-like" priors/expectancy are two sides of one mechanism (EV-0010, EV-0011, EV-0012, EV-0014; C-0004), and even a leading non-state theorist (Wagstaff 2026) now argues there is "no necessary contradiction" between an altered state and its arising from ordinary processes (EV-0034).

**Practically**: the state/non-state distinction has no demonstrated bearing on clinical efficacy. Across 49 meta-analyses / 261 RCTs, hypnosis shows reliably positive effects (99.2% positive; >50% medium-or-larger) for pain, procedural distress, anxiety, etc., and the only consistent moderator is hypnotizability (r=0.24), not theoretical model (EV-0033; C-0008). The debate is primarily explanatory/theoretical, not clinical.

## What is established vs contested vs genuinely unresolved

### Established (high confidence, verified)
- Hypnosis is accompanied by measurable, replicable changes in brain activity and connectivity (C-0001, EV-0001..0004, EV-0019).
- No hypnosis-specific unique physiological fingerprint has been identified; induction effects are small (C-0002, EV-0005..0009, EV-0016, EV-0020).
- The strict "trance required" hypothesis is ruled out (C-0005, EV-0008, EV-0016).
- DLPFC inhibition causally increases hypnotizability, beyond expectancy (C-0007, EV-0029..0031).
- Hypnosis is clinically efficacious (especially pain, procedures, children); hypnotizability moderates outcome (C-0008, EV-0033).

### Contested (verified claims under active interpretation)
- Whether the brain changes are *causal* of responsiveness or *epiphenomenal* (the HIGH interpretive conflict — EV-0028). Cycle-2 causal evidence (C-0007) weakens the pure-epiphenomenal reading but does not eliminate it.
- Whether the field's lack of consensus (C-0003, EV-0013, EV-0018) reflects genuine underdetermination or two research camps talking past each other (SQ-PR1).

### Genuinely unresolved (declared permanent dispute per guard 3, redirected)
- Which specific mechanism is correct: cold-control vs dissociated-control vs SATH/predictive-coding vs ability-based/phenomenological-control (C-0006, C-0007, EV-0015, EV-0017, EV-0018). The leading theories make overlapping predictions on the manipulations tested so far; the discriminating experiments (precision-weighting manipulations, multi-node causal designs) are as-yet-undone or inconclusive.
- Trait vs state nature of hypnotizability: baseline EEG (trait) and post-induction dynamics (state) markers coexist (EV-0019).
